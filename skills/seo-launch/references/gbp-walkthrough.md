# Google Business Profile — video verification script

When GBP requires video verification (increasingly the default for
service-area businesses), film one continuous 30-60 second take that
includes ALL of:

## Shot list

1. **Face shot (5 sec)** — camera up to your face: "I'm [Name], [role]
   at [Business], in [Country]." Required — without a face shot, ~30%
   resubmission rate.
2. **Workspace pan (10 sec)** — show real office/desk, computer, real
   clutter. Not a staged clean room.
3. **Website URL (10 sec)** — pull up `https://yoursite.com` in browser,
   show the URL bar.
4. **Business email (10 sec)** — open Outlook/Gmail showing inbox titled
   with the business name, real vendor emails visible.
5. **Closing shot (10 sec)** — workspace + your face again, say site URL
   or contact.

## Filming tips

- Vertical phone video at native 1080x1920 or 720x1280
- Daylight or window light
- One continuous take, no cuts
- 30-90 seconds total (60 is the sweet spot)
- Real workspace, not staged
- No other people in frame

## Submission

Upload via the GBP prompt. Google reviews within 24-72 hours typically.
You'll get an email when verified.
